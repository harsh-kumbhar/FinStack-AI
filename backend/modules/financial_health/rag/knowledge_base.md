# Financial Health Score

## Definition

The Financial Health Score is a numerical indicator representing the overall financial condition of a user.

The score ranges from 0 to 100.

Higher scores indicate better financial stability and healthier financial habits.

---

## Score Interpretation

Excellent
80 - 100

Good
60 - 79

Average
40 - 59

Poor
0 - 39

---

## Factors Affecting Score

The Financial Health Score is influenced by multiple financial indicators including:

• Savings Rate

• Debt-to-Income Ratio

• Emergency Fund

• Investment Ratio

• Expense Ratio

• Monthly Cashflow

Each metric contributes differently towards the overall score.

---

# Savings Rate

## Definition

Savings Rate represents the percentage of monthly income that is saved instead of spent.

---

## Formula

Savings Rate = Monthly Savings / Monthly Income

---

## Recommended Value

30% or higher

---

## Why It Matters

A higher savings rate improves financial stability.

It provides funds for emergencies, future goals and wealth creation.

---

## Low Savings Rate

Possible Reasons

• High monthly expenses

• Lifestyle inflation

• Poor budgeting

• Unplanned spending

---

## Improvement Tips

• Reduce unnecessary expenses

• Create a monthly budget

• Automate savings

• Increase income where possible

---

# Debt-to-Income Ratio

## Definition

Debt-to-Income Ratio measures how much of a person's income is used to repay debt.

---

## Formula

Total Monthly Debt Payments / Monthly Income

---

## Recommended Value

Below 35%

---

## Why It Matters

Lower debt improves financial flexibility and reduces financial stress.

High debt may negatively affect long-term financial stability.

---

## Improvement Tips

• Pay high-interest debt first

• Avoid unnecessary borrowing

• Increase income

• Reduce discretionary spending

---

# Emergency Fund

## Definition

Emergency Fund represents money reserved for unexpected financial situations.

Examples include:

• Medical emergencies

• Job loss

• Emergency repairs

---

## Recommended Value

At least six months of living expenses.

---

## Why It Matters

A healthy emergency fund protects users from relying on loans during emergencies.

---

## Improvement Tips

• Save consistently every month

• Keep emergency savings separate

• Avoid using the fund for regular expenses

---

# Investment Ratio

## Definition

Investment Ratio measures the portion of income allocated toward investments.

---

## Recommended Value

20% or higher

---

## Why It Matters

Regular investments support long-term wealth creation through disciplined financial planning.

---

## Improvement Tips

• Invest consistently

• Start with small amounts

• Review investments periodically

---

# Expense Ratio

## Definition

Expense Ratio measures how much of monthly income is spent on monthly expenses.

---

## Formula

Monthly Expenses / Monthly Income

---

## Recommended Value

Below 50%

---

## Why It Matters

Lower expenses allow greater savings and investment opportunities.

---

## Improvement Tips

• Track spending

• Reduce discretionary expenses

• Build a monthly budget

---

# Monthly Cashflow

## Definition

Cashflow is the money remaining after monthly expenses.

---

## Formula

Monthly Income − Monthly Expenses

---

## Positive Cashflow

Positive cashflow indicates that income exceeds expenses.

---

## Negative Cashflow

Negative cashflow indicates expenses exceed income and may require immediate financial planning.

---
