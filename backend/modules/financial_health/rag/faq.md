# FinStack AI Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

# General Questions

## What is FinStack AI?

FinStack AI is an AI-powered Personal Finance and Financial Decision Intelligence Platform that analyzes a user's financial profile to generate a Financial Health Score, identify financial strengths and weaknesses, provide personalized recommendations, and answer finance-related questions using an AI chatbot.

---

## What is the purpose of FinStack AI?

The purpose of FinStack AI is to improve financial awareness by helping users understand their financial condition, identify financial risks, and make better financial decisions.

---

## Is FinStack AI free to use?

The current version of FinStack AI is developed as an educational and academic project. Future versions may include additional features and deployment options.

---

## Is my financial data secure?

FinStack AI is designed to securely process financial information. Users should always follow good security practices and avoid sharing confidential information unnecessarily.

---

# Financial Health Score

## What is a Financial Health Score?

The Financial Health Score is a machine learning generated score between 0 and 100 that represents a user's overall financial health.

---

## How is the Financial Health Score calculated?

The score is predicted using a trained machine learning model that considers savings, debt, emergency funds, investments, expenses, cashflow, insurance, employment status, financial goals, and financial persona.

---

## Why is my Financial Health Score low?

A low score may result from factors such as:

• High debt

• Low savings

• High expenses

• Poor emergency fund

• Low investments

• Negative cashflow

---

## What is considered a good Financial Health Score?

80–100 : Excellent

60–79 : Good

40–59 : Average

0–39 : Poor

---

## Can my Financial Health Score improve?

Yes.

Improving savings, reducing debt, increasing investments, controlling expenses, and maintaining positive cashflow can gradually improve your score.

---

# Savings

## What is Savings Rate?

Savings Rate is the percentage of monthly income that is saved.

---

## Why is Savings Rate important?

Higher savings increase financial stability and help achieve future financial goals.

---

## What is a good Savings Rate?

Generally:

Below 10% : Poor

10–20% : Average

20–30% : Good

Above 30% : Excellent

---

## How can I increase my Savings Rate?

• Reduce unnecessary spending

• Prepare a monthly budget

• Automate savings

• Increase income

---

# Debt

## What is Debt-to-Income Ratio?

Debt-to-Income Ratio measures debt relative to annual income.

---

## Why is high debt bad?

High debt reduces financial flexibility and increases financial risk.

---

## How can I reduce debt?

• Repay high-interest loans first

• Avoid unnecessary borrowing

• Increase monthly repayments

• Improve budgeting

---

# Emergency Fund

## What is an Emergency Fund?

Emergency savings reserved for unexpected situations.

---

## How much Emergency Fund should I have?

At least six months of essential monthly expenses.

Individuals with irregular income may require larger emergency funds.

---

## Why is an Emergency Fund important?

It prevents financial stress and reduces dependence on loans during emergencies.

---

# Investments

## Why should I invest?

Investing helps grow wealth, beat inflation, and achieve long-term financial goals.

---

## When should I start investing?

As early as possible.

Long-term investing benefits from compounding.

---

## What if I cannot invest much?

Small, regular investments are generally better than waiting until larger amounts become available.

---

# Expenses

## What is Expense Ratio?

Expense Ratio measures the percentage of monthly income spent every month.

---

## How can I reduce my monthly expenses?

• Track spending

• Reduce discretionary expenses

• Cancel unnecessary subscriptions

• Compare prices before purchasing

---

# Cashflow

## What is Monthly Cashflow?

Monthly Cashflow is the money remaining after monthly expenses and savings.

---

## Why is positive cashflow important?

Positive cashflow allows better savings, investments, and financial flexibility.

---

# Insurance

## Why do I need insurance?

Insurance protects individuals and families against unexpected financial losses.

---

## Which insurance should I consider?

Common types include:

• Health Insurance

• Life Insurance

• Vehicle Insurance

• Home Insurance

# AI Chatbot

## How does the chatbot answer my questions?

The chatbot combines your financial report with the FinStack AI Knowledge Base using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide personalized responses.

---

## Can the chatbot see my financial report?

Yes.

It uses your current financial report to provide context-aware answers.

---

## Does the chatbot give financial advice?

The chatbot provides educational guidance and personalized financial insights.

It should not be considered a replacement for professional financial or investment advice.

---

Users may choose to store reports to monitor financial progress over time.

Historical reports help identify long-term financial trends.

---

# Technical Questions

## Is the Financial Health Score generated using Artificial Intelligence?

Yes.

The score is predicted using a trained machine learning regression model combined with rule-based financial analysis.

---

## Does FinStack AI use Large Language Models?

Yes.

Large Language Models are used to generate AI summaries and answer financial questions through the chatbot.

---

## What is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines a knowledge base with a Large Language Model so that answers are grounded in relevant financial information instead of relying only on the model's internal knowledge.

---

# Disclaimer

## Should I rely only on FinStack AI for financial decisions?

No.

FinStack AI is intended for educational and informational purposes. Users should consult qualified financial professionals before making significant financial, investment, legal, or tax-related decisions.
